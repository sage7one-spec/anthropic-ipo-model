How it works. Day 1: each of 10,000 paths samples a closing market cap from the live Polymarket Day-1 close-cap buckets, converts it to $/share with the FD share count, and re-centers it toward the blend of the Hyperliquid xyz:ANT mark and the Polymarket-implied price. The open is drawn from historical mega-IPO open-to-close returns; a Brownian bridge connects open to close in 8 hourly steps, with interval extremes sampled in closed form so touches, stops and fills are detected continuously. The 1-year engine walks every Day-1 close forward with decaying volatility, the deterministic lock-up overhang, earnings jumps, and a draw from the fundamental scenario mixture. The SpaceX engine underneath is unchanged and keeps its 62 unit tests; the ANT extensions add 14 more, all on verbatim captures of the live payloads.

Stops are modeled as filling at the stop price; real first-day fills can gap through. Tax figures are simplified (a loss is credited at the same rate) — check them against your own situation.
